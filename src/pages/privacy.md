**Who we are.** tattva-3d is a small 3D printing studio run by Shameek Vats, Aalborg, Denmark. Contact: t-3d@ik.me.

**What we collect.** When you use the order form we receive your name, email, optional phone number, order details, and any photos or files you send us.

**Why.** Only to answer your request, make your order, and arrange pickup and payment.

**Who else processes it.** Form submissions are delivered via Formspree (formspree.io). Photos you email are stored in our email account. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use advertising trackers on this site.

**Your photos.** We use your photos only to make your order. We will not post your product or photo on Instagram or this website without asking you first.

**How long.** We delete order emails and photos 12 months after your order is completed, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

**Your rights.** Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing t-3d@ik.me. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).
